Make technical investment compound

As AI moves from answering questions to taking action, teams need reusable foundations, controlled costs and verifiable delivery. These are the outcomes we aim to support.

Delivery
Reduce repeated integration and environment setup so teams can focus on their work.
Economics
Connect model usage, task budgets and environment lifecycles to make costs visible.
Trust
Build permissions, execution evidence and result verification into workflows.
Reuse
Share skills, knowledge, templates and practices while preserving choice and portability.

From open components to a connected ecosystem

We expect the work to move from individual tools toward connected systems. Each step should build on working software and lessons from its users.

Select a direction to explore the work

Useful foundations

Build model access, execution, asset management and verification around real needs. Each component should stand on its own.

Real use and community feedback inform every stage.

This diagram describes our intended direction, not a release schedule or a statement of completed capabilities. Work in these areas may overlap; priorities will follow actual needs and available contributions.

How we approach the work

  • Keep the boundaries clearPrefer small, composable capabilities with understandable interfaces.
  • Make the work usableDocumentation, examples, and maintainability matter alongside implementation.
  • Show what worksBe clear about what is available, what has been verified, and what is still being explored.
